# Power BI Dashboard

## E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics

This folder documents the Power BI dashboard developed to analyze e-commerce sales, customers, products, profitability, operations, and marketing performance.

### Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Overview** — Revenue, profit, orders, customers, and sales trends.
2. **Customer & Sales Analytics** — Customer segments, repeat purchasing, sales channels, and customer lifetime value.
3. **Product Analytics** — Product sales, units sold, costs, profit, and margins.
4. **Operations & Delivery** — Cancellations, returns, delivery performance, and warehouse operations.
5. **Profitability & Risk** — Profit margins, loss-making orders, and profitability analysis.
6. **Marketing & Customer Satisfaction** — Marketing channels, customer ratings, and review sentiment.

### Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query for data preparation
- DAX for measures and KPIs
- Data modeling and relationships
- Interactive visualizations and filters
- PostgreSQL as the data source

- `dashboard.pdf` — Exported dashboard preview, if included in this folder.
